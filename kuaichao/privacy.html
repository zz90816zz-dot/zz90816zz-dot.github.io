快炒店老闆 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 20 日

一、遊戲進度

你的店名、遊戲幣、食材、升級等進度只存在你的手機上。在設定裡按「重置遊戲」就會全部清除。

二、富豪榜

連上網路時，遊戲會把店名、當天賺到的遊戲幣、當天服務的客人數上傳到每日富豪榜，其他玩家看得到你的店名和成績。為了分辨是哪一台手機，遊戲會在 Google Firebase 自動建立一個匿名帳號（不需要登入，也不會拿到你的姓名、電話或 Google 帳號）。請不要把真實姓名或聯絡方式取成店名。

三、廣告

「好康廣告」使用 Google AdMob 播放獎勵廣告，只有你自己選擇觀看時才會播。AdMob 可能會收集你的裝置廣告 ID、大概位置（由 IP 位址推估）、App 使用情形與診斷資料，用來提供及衡量廣告、防範詐欺。這些資料由 Google 依其隱私權政策處理（https://policies.google.com/privacy）。歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士的使用者會先看到同意視窗，之後也可以在遊戲的「設定 → 廣告隱私設定」隨時變更。

四、儲值

儲值透過 Google Play 帳款服務完成，付款資料（例如信用卡）由 Google 處理，我們收不到。遊戲只會收到購買成功的確認，用來把遊戲幣加進你的存檔。

五、手機權限

網路（富豪榜、廣告、儲值）、顯示在其他 App 上層與通知（營業中切到別的 App 時的懸浮球提醒）、震動。這些權限都不會用來收集個人資料。

六、兒童

本遊戲不是為 13 歲以下兒童設計的，也不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。

七、刪除資料與聯絡我們

想刪除富豪榜上的資料，或對本政策有任何問題，請來信：zz90816zz@gmail.com。政策如有變更，會更新本頁並修改上方日期。

Kuaichao Boss — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Game progress

Your shop name, in-game money, ingredients and upgrades are stored only on your device. "Reset game" in Settings deletes all of it.

2. Daily leaderboard

When you are online, the game uploads your shop name, the in-game money earned that day and the number of customers served to the daily leaderboard, where other players can see your shop name and score. To tell devices apart, the game automatically creates an anonymous account in Google Firebase (no sign-in; we never receive your name, phone number or Google account). Please do not use your real name or contact details as your shop name.

3. Ads

The game shows rewarded ads through Google AdMob, only when you choose to watch one. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), app usage and diagnostics to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. This data is handled by Google under its privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first and can change it at any time in Settings → Ad privacy settings.

4. Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Payment details (such as card numbers) are handled by Google and never reach us; the game only receives a confirmation so it can add the in-game money to your save.

5. Permissions

Internet (leaderboard, ads, purchases), display over other apps and notifications (the floating reminder while your shop is open), and vibration. None of these are used to collect personal data.

6. Children

This game is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Deleting data and contact

To delete your leaderboard data or ask about this policy, email zz90816zz@gmail.com. If this policy changes, this page and the date above will be updated.
